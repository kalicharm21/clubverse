# Product Overview

CampusVerse centralizes college club discovery, event workflows, attendance verification, and institutional reporting into a single web and Progressive Web App (PWA).

## Core Problem Solved

Universities often run clubs across fragmented spreadsheets, chat groups, and paper attendance sheets. CampusVerse connects:
- **Students** seeking structured club involvement without spam (capped at 2 followed clubs).
- **Club Teams** needing dynamic registration forms, single-use QR gate check-in, and post-event reporting.
- **Student Affairs / Super Admins** requiring transparent event, financial, and audit visibility.

## Explicit Product Boundaries

- No event photo galleries (intentionally excluded).
- Club financial reports are strictly private to authorized club officers and Super Admins—never visible to students.
- QR codes contain only opaque tokens (`CV-QR-...`) and zero personal information.
